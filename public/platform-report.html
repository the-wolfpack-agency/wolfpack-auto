1 Executive Summary

Wolfpack Auto is a dealer website platform with an invisible intelligence layer. While customers see a clean car shopping experience, 31 separate systems analyze their behavior in real time — detecting interest, hesitation, frustration, and purchase intent. What makes this different: A normal dealer website is a brochure. This platform is a brochure that watches, learns, and reports back. It knows which vehicles get the most attention, which customers are comparison shopping, which page elements frustrate people, and whether your marketing spend is actually working. All of this happens automatically, with zero manual effort.

2 How a Customer Visit Works

Every step of a customer's visit is tracked and analyzed. Here's what happens behind the scenes:

1. Customer ArrivesPage view recorded. Traffic source identified (Google, Facebook, direct). Device and location noted.
↓
2. Customer SearchesSearch terms captured. If we don't have what they searched for, that's logged as an inventory gap opportunity.
↓
3. Customer Views VehiclesTime on each vehicle tracked. Photo interactions measured. If they view 3+ vehicles, comparison shopping is detected.
↓
4. Customer Uses ChatAI chat answers questions using real inventory data. Every message analyzed for purchase intent.
↓
5. Customer Fills FormLead captured. Lead Temperature Score calculated from ALL previous signals. Scored as hot, warm, cool, or cold.
↓
6. Data Stored in 3 PlacesEvery event saved to PostgreSQL (permanent record), Qdrant (AI search), and Neo4j (journey mapping). Zero data loss.

3 The 31 Insight Generators

These systems run automatically on every visit. They detect patterns that would be impossible for a human to track manually.

Engagement — 13 Insights

Understanding how visitors interact with your site.

1. Page Engagement Ranking

Which pages get the most attention? Reveals your best-performing content.

2. Vehicle Interest

Which vehicles get the most views and longest viewing time? Shows real demand.

3. Peak Hours

When are your customers most active? Informs staffing and ad scheduling.

4. Copy Behavior

What text do visitors copy? Copying a VIN = serious buyer. Copying a price = comparison shopping.

5. Scroll Behavior

Are visitors skimmers (barely scrolling), readers, or deep-divers? Tells you if content length is right.

6. Time-to-First-Interaction

How fast do visitors take action? Under 3 seconds = focused buyer. Over 10 seconds = hesitant.

7. Session Momentum

Is the visitor getting more engaged or losing interest as they browse? Detects the tipping point.

8. Viewport Attention

What content visitors actually read vs what they scroll past. Reveals what matters to buyers.

9. Photo Engagement Score

Per-vehicle score based on gallery views, zoom, swipe, and time on photos.

10. Gallery Engagement

Detailed photo interaction data — which specific photos get the most attention per vehicle.

11. Device Orientation

Mobile users rotating their phone to landscape for photos = high interest signal.

12. Return Visit Sticky Pages

Which pages bring people back for a second or third visit? Those pages close deals.

13. Cross-Page Journey Patterns

The exact path visitors take through your site — stored as a visual journey map.

Conversion — 13 Insights

Detecting purchase intent and removing obstacles to inquiry.

1. Conversion Paths

What sequence of pages leads to a sale inquiry? Reveals your winning formula.

2. Micro-Hesitation Patterns

Hovering on a price without clicking, viewing the same vehicle 3 times — classic signs of an undecided buyer who needs a nudge.

3. Comparison Shopping Detection

Viewing 3+ vehicles in one session = actively comparing. You can show them a comparison tool.

4. Form Abandonment

Which form field makes people give up? If 40% quit at "phone number," maybe make it optional.

5. Exit Intent

Detects when someone moves their mouse toward the browser's close button — last chance to show an offer.

6. Price Trajectory

Tracks what price ranges attract each visitor over time. Shows the budget they're gravitating toward.

7. Lead Temperature Score

The master score. Combines ALL signals — pages viewed, time on site, vehicles checked, chat usage, form interactions — into a single hot/warm/cool/cold rating for every visitor.

8. Cross-Session Buyer Lifecycle

Tracks visitors across multiple days. Detects the progression: first visit (browsing) → second visit (considering) → third visit (ready to buy).

9. Performance → Conversion Correlation

Does a slow-loading page actually cost you sales? This measures it with real data.

10. CTA Visibility Gap

Are your "Schedule Test Drive" buttons actually being seen? If they're below the fold, visitors might never see them.

11. Vehicle Comparison Patterns

Which vehicles get compared together most often? Informs how you group and recommend inventory.

12. Financing Intent Profile

Detects visitors researching payments, trade-in values, and credit — even without filling out a financing form.

13. Return Visit Stickiness

Which pages keep bringing visitors back until they finally convert? Those are your closers.

Search — 2 Insights

1. Search Behavior

What people search for, what they click from results, and searches that return nothing. Every dead-end search is a missed sale.

2. Inventory Gap Detection

Compares what people search for against what you actually have. "15 people searched for Honda Civic this week and you don't have one" — that's an actionable buying signal.

Chat — 3 Insights

1. Chat Engagement

Tracks message count, topics discussed, response satisfaction, and whether chat conversations lead to form submissions or calls.

2. Chat-to-Conversion Attribution

Did the chat conversation actually lead to a form submission or call? Measures the real conversion rate of chat users vs non-chat users. Answers: "Is the chat closing deals or just answering questions?"

3. Chat Topic Demand

What topics come up most in chat? "financing" "trade-in" "availability" — reveals what information is missing from your pages. If 40% of chats ask about trade-ins, you need a trade-in page.

UX Friction — 4 Insights

Detecting when visitors are frustrated or confused.

1. Rage Clicks

3+ rapid clicks in the same spot = something is broken or confusing. Automatically flagged for review.

2. Dead Clicks

Clicking on something that isn't a button or link. Visitors expected it to be interactive — a design problem.

3. Slow Page Bounce Correlation

Pages that load slow AND have high bounce rates = direct revenue loss. Shows exactly which pages to speed up first, ranked by business impact — not just speed scores.

4. Mobile Pinch-Zoom Detection

Users pinching to zoom on mobile means text or buttons are too small. Pinpoints exactly which pages have sizing issues so you can fix them.

Marketing — 4 Insights

1. Referrer Behavior Correlation

Do visitors from Google behave differently than visitors from Facebook or Instagram ads? Shows which marketing channels bring buyers vs browsers.

2. UTM Campaign Attribution

Tracks every ad campaign (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign) through to conversion. Shows exactly which campaigns generate leads vs just traffic. Stop guessing which ads to keep running.

3. Retargeting Signals

Identifies visitors who viewed vehicles 3+ times without converting — prime candidates for retargeting ads. Shows their top vehicle of interest and visit frequency.

4. Seasonal Demand Patterns

What vehicle types are trending by month? SUV searches spike before winter, convertible interest rises in spring. Plan your inventory 30-60 days ahead of the demand curve.

Triple-Write Storage

Every event is written to three databases simultaneously. Each serves a different purpose:

PostgreSQL — The Permanent Record

Every single event stored with full detail: timestamp, session, user, page, device, location, referrer. Think of it as the dealership's complete memory. Nothing is ever deleted. You can always go back and answer "what happened last Tuesday?"

Qdrant — The AI Brain

Events and vehicle data are converted into mathematical representations that AI can understand. This powers the chat widget's ability to answer questions like "show me SUVs under $45k with low mileage" and find vehicles by meaning, not just keywords.

Neo4j — The Journey Map

Every visitor's path through the site is stored as a connected graph. You can literally see: "Customer A viewed the homepage, searched for BMW, looked at 3 vehicles, used chat, then submitted a lead form." This reveals which journeys lead to conversions.

1. Dynamic Meta Tags

Every page automatically gets the perfect title and description for Google search results. No manual SEO work needed.

2. Vehicle Rich Results

Your vehicles can appear in Google with price, mileage, and photos directly in search results — not just a plain link. This is done via structured data (JSON-LD) generated automatically for every vehicle.

3. Sitemap Generation

A complete map of your site is generated and kept updated so Google always knows about every page and every vehicle listing.

4. Page Speed Monitoring

Google penalizes slow sites. This tracks Core Web Vitals (loading speed, interactivity, visual stability) to make sure your site stays fast.

5. High-Value Page Identification

Which pages drive the most business? Those pages get priority in search optimization and content investment.

6. Popular Search Terms

What people search for on your site tells you what content to create. If 50 people search for "certified pre-owned" and you don't have a page for it, that's a gap.

7. Behavior-Optimized Sitemap

A unique innovation: your sitemap prioritizes pages based on actual visitor behavior, not just a static list. Pages that real people care about get higher priority with search engines.

13 Compliance & Security

The platform is built to meet regulatory requirements for auto dealers, data privacy, and web security — out of the box.

Privacy & Data Protection

GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Full privacy policy covering data collection, retention periods, and user rights. European visitors get GDPR-specific rights (access, erasure, portability). California residents get CCPA protections (right to know, right to delete, right to opt out).

Cookie Consent

Visitors choose "Accept All" or "Essential Only." When essential-only is selected, behavioral tracking is disabled — only basic page views are recorded. Full transparency, full control.

Data Deletion on Request

Any customer can request their data be deleted. A dedicated API endpoint removes all personal information from all databases within one request. 72-hour breach notification commitment documented in policy.

PII Encryption

All personal information (emails, phone numbers) is encrypted before storage using AES-256-GCM — the same standard used by banks. Even if the database were compromised, personal data remains unreadable.

Auto Dealer Regulations

FTC Buyer's Guide Compliance

Every vehicle listing displays: condition badge (New/Used/Certified Pre-Owned), odometer disclosure, warranty status ("As-Is" or warranty details), and price disclaimers (excluding tax, title, registration).

FTC CARS Rule (2024)

Full compliance with the Combatting Auto Retail Scams Rule. No surprise fees — the price you see is the price you pay. All financing terms disclosed before signing. Collapsible disclosure on every vehicle page.

Truth-in-Lending (Regulation Z)

All financing estimates include required disclaimers: "Subject to credit approval," rate disclaimers, APR definitions, and clear notes that estimates exclude tax, title, registration, and insurance.

Platform Security

Admin Authentication

All dealer admin pages require secure login. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions expire after 8 hours or 30 minutes of inactivity. Login is protected against brute force attacks (5-attempt lockout).

Security Headers

Every page response includes: HSTS (force HTTPS), Content Security Policy, X-Frame-Options (prevent clickjacking), XSS protection, and restrictive permissions policy. CSRF protection on all forms.

Audit Logging

Every administrative action is logged with timestamp, user, and details. Creates an immutable record for compliance audits and security investigations.

AI Transparency

AI-generated content is clearly labeled. Chat widget displays "AI Assistant — responses may not be fully accurate." Vehicle descriptions generated by AI are marked as such. Full transparency with customers.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 Level AA

The platform targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards. Keyboard navigation, screen reader support, semantic HTML, ARIA labels, focus indicators, and responsive design are all built in. Accessibility statement published at /accessibility.
