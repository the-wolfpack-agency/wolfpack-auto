## Summary

**Where Wolfpack matches Tekion:** Every core DMS function — desking, accounting, payments, payroll, service, inventory, multi-company GL, lender management, credit bureau integration.

**Where Wolfpack goes beyond Tekion:**
- Full CRM with predictive lead scoring (Tekion explicitly lacks a CRM)
- 80+ behavioral analytics signals that no other DMS collects
- AI pricing engine with 9 calculation modules
- Vehicle photo background studio with AI compositing
- Dealer website included in the platform
- Marketing templates and syndication feeds
- 29 automated compliance checks + OFAC screening
- A/B testing framework
- Dramatically lower cost

**Where Tekion has the edge:**
- Security certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) — in progress for Wolfpack
- OEM partnerships (Toyota, Kia) — requires business development
- 6+ years of production usage at scale
