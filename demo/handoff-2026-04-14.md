## Dealer onboarding flow (NEW — end-to-end)

```
Login (/admin/login)
  ↓ (2FA if MFA enabled)
Middleware check: onboarding complete?
  ↓ NO → redirect to /admin/getting-started
Getting Started Checklist (4 steps with progress)
  ↓ click "Start Setup"
Onboarding Wizard (/admin/onboarding)
  Step 1: Dealership Info (name, address, phone, email, website)
  Step 2: Customize (logo, colors, tagline)
  Step 3: Invite Your Team (email + role, add multiple, skip option)
  Step 4: Review & Launch
  ↓ submit
Dashboard (/admin) — banner hidden, full features unlocked
```
