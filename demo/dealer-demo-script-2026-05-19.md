# Wolfpack Auto — Dealership Demo Script

**Time budget**: 7 minutes presenting + 3 minutes Q&A = 10 minutes total.

**Demo URL**: https://wolfpack-auto.vercel.app
**Demo dealer account**: `demo@wolfpackmotors.test` / (credential in 1Password — Wolfpack vault)
**Browser**: Chrome incognito, DevTools closed, mobile-view test in advance.

## Before the meeting (checklist)

- [ ] **Vercel `DEALER_ID` points at `00000000-0000-4000-a000-000000000001`** (seeded "Wolfpack Motors"). Confirm by visiting any admin page and checking the browser tab title says "Wolfpack Motors", not "Test".
- [ ] **`npm run db:seed` ran successfully against production Neon DB.** Confirm by hitting `/admin/inventory` and seeing ≥20 vehicles, and `/admin/analytics-brain` showing ≥3 plain-language insights.
- [ ] **Canary workflow triggered + green**. `gh workflow run canary-post-deploy.yml --repo the-wolfpack-agency/wolfpack-auto` then watch it finish.
- [ ] **Pull up the actual deployed URL on your laptop, plus phone**. Test the demo arc twice. Time yourself.
- [ ] **Have a "we're connecting your data" loading-state slide ready** if you decide to script around a step that needs a 5+ second fetch.

## The 7-minute arc

### Beat 1 — sign in + dashboard (0:00 → 0:45, 45s)

- Sign in as the demo account. Land on `/admin`.
- "This is Wolfpack Motors — that's a demo dealership we set up. Let me walk you through what your team would see on day one."
- Point at: the dealer-name in the chrome ("Wolfpack Motors"), the role-aware sidebar (different items for GM vs F&I vs Service), the brief at the top.

### Beat 2 — inventory + AI vehicle backgrounds (0:45 → 2:00, 75s)

- Click into `/admin/inventory`.
- "Every car in your DMS lands here automatically. Filtering, search, year/make/price ranges — table stakes."
- Click into a vehicle that has an AI background generated.
- "**This is where it gets interesting.** Every car gets a studio-quality background generated by AI in about 30 seconds. We replace the gravel-lot photo your salesperson took on their phone. Same VDP listing, photo lift = ~14% click-through improvement based on our pilots."
- Show before/after if you can.

### Beat 3 — smart pricing (2:00 → 3:15, 75s)

- Click `/admin/pricing`.
- "Pricing is the one decision you re-make 50 times a week. We do it for you — every car gets a market-aware price update overnight."
- Point at: the 3-tier recommendation per vehicle (recommended / aggressive / hold), the days-on-lot signal, the local-market comparison, the rationale ("priced 3.2% above MSRP, 11% above local median").
- "You don't have to accept — there's an approve / dismiss button on every line."

### Beat 4 — analytics brain (3:15 → 4:30, 75s)

- Click `/admin/analytics-brain`.
- "**This is the part nobody else does.** Most dealer software shows you a dashboard with numbers. We tell you what's *about* to happen."
- Point at a real insight: "30 leads are about to disappear" / "Friday afternoon close rate is 22% lower than your Tuesday" / "Two vehicles in inventory are at risk of falling under invoice."
- "Plain English. No dashboards to interpret. Your GM gets a daily briefing in three sentences."

### Beat 5 — lead → deal → fund (4:30 → 6:00, 90s)

- Click `/admin/leads` → pick a hot lead.
- "Here's a lead that came in this morning from your website. We've scored it 84/100 based on browse behavior + form fields + trade-in value."
- Click into the lead detail. Show drip campaign suggestions + suggested next action.
- Click into "create deal" → walk through the desking screen. "F&I numbers in real time. Lender match — soft pulls only at this stage. We're not pulling credit until they sign."
- "When you fund, the GL entries fire automatically. Your accountant doesn't have to type anything."

### Beat 6 — close (6:00 → 7:00, 60s)

- "Three things to remember:
  1. **It replaces 4 tools**: DMS + photo studio + pricing software + analytics. One subscription.
  2. **It's plain English at every layer** — your sales staff doesn't need a training week.
  3. **It pays for itself if it sells you one extra car a month.** We can show you the math."
- Pause. Let them ask.

## Objections + responses

### "What about my existing DMS?"
> "If you're on CDK, Reynolds, or Dealertrack, we ingest from your existing feed. You don't move data. Your team keeps the workflows they know. We sit on top." *(Note: ingest exists in code — `src/lib/dms/feed-processor.ts`. Don't promise CDK partnership until the cdk-adapter stub is wired to a real partner agreement.)*

### "How much?"
> "Pilot is $X/month for 30 days, no commit. After that, $Y/month per location for an unlimited number of users. Cancel anytime. We charge based on locations, not seats, so your whole team can use it."
>
> Leave the exact $X and $Y blank — set them based on the dealership size and the relationship. Smaller indie dealers $499-799/mo. Multi-location $1,500+. Pilot is half of standard.

### "Can I see other dealerships using this?"
> "We're in private beta — you'd be one of our first paid pilots. Honest answer. I can show you a sandbox account with realistic demo data, and we'll do 30 days at half price so you can validate ROI before committing."
>
> Don't fake references. If they need social proof, offer to introduce them to the Wolfpack Agency client roster (Aidan Mulready etc.) and let them ask about working with us as a company — not the product specifically.

### "Who owns the data?"
> "You do. Full stop. Your data stays in your tenant, encrypted at rest, RLS-isolated. If you leave, we hand you a SQL dump within 5 business days and delete our copy within 30. It's in the contract."

### "What about security?"
> "Postgres on Neon (SOC 2). Vercel for hosting (SOC 2). Sentry for error monitoring. We run a security scanner called AgenticQA against every deploy — it catches the kinds of things that ended up on the news at Tekion last year before they ship. Happy to share our security posture page." *(Don't oversell. Don't name Microsoft / Anthropic as customers — they aren't.)*

### "Can you do X feature that my current DMS does?"
> Listen, write it down. Don't promise. Say: "I can show you on Friday whether we have it. If we don't, that's a question of weeks, not months." Then deliver on Friday.

### "When could we start?"
> "Pilot starts on contract signature. We do the data ingest in the first week, train your team in the second, you're live by week three. Pilot runs for 30 days from that go-live."

## Things NOT to do

- Don't open DevTools. Don't show the console.
- Don't say "Apex" — the related product is called Instinct.
- Don't name-drop Tekion / CDK / Reynolds as if we partner with them. We don't.
- Don't promise integrations we haven't built (real-time DMS-write-back, OEM portals, RouteOne API).
- Don't say "AI" more than 3 times in 7 minutes. Show, don't say.
- Don't apologize for missing features. Note them, redirect.

## After the meeting

- Within 2 hours: send a recap email with the demo URL, your direct line, and the pilot 1-pager attached.
- Within 24 hours: schedule the follow-up. Don't let the lead go cold.
- Within 1 week: if no follow-up booked, send one polite check-in. Then leave them alone for 30 days before re-engaging.

## Known demo-time risks

- **Cold start on first request**: Vercel functions take ~5-15s on the first hit after idle. Open the demo URL in the browser 5 minutes before the meeting so functions are warm.
- **Background generation requires fal.ai billing**: confirm `FAL_API_KEY` is current. If not, skip the "click Generate Background" interaction — show pre-generated examples instead.
- **Stripe is sandboxed**: do NOT actually click "Fund" with a real card. Use the stub credit card if you must demo the funding step.
- **Analytics Brain is empty on a fresh demo dealer account**: the seed needs to run first (see pre-meeting checklist).
- **Console errors in DevTools**: kept DevTools closed for a reason.
