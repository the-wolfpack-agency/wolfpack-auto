# OneMagnify Partnership Brief

**Date:** 2026-05-11
**For:** Wolfpack CEO (warm intro lead)
**Prepared by:** Nick Homyk, CTO
**Source data:** OneMagnify public site, April 2026 leadership announcements

---

## Why this is a strong fit

OneMagnify is a marketing services agency that positions itself as "AI-native" and explicitly markets data, analytics, AI, performance marketing, digital experience, eCommerce, and channel-partner solutions. Public signals:

- **FordDirect is a named client** (AI-powered chat intelligence). Direct relationship into the auto-dealer network.
- **Tech-stack partnerships:** Domo, Databricks, Salesforce, Shopify, Optimizely, BigCommerce. They run heavy AI / analytics infrastructure.
- **New CEO appointed April 2026** (Ravi Karumuru). Brand-new leadership is usually receptive to partnerships that make them look strategic in their first 90 days.
- **India Global Capability Center opened April 2026.** They are investing in scale and AI capacity right now.
- **Visible gap:** No mention of creative production, deep custom engineering, or vertical SaaS in their core offering. Their AI focus is marketing / analytics, not workflow operations or compliance.

These signals point to five concrete partnership plays.

---

## The five plays, ranked

### Play 1: Wolfpack Instinct as their internal AI cost-efficiency layer (immediate ROI, low friction)

**The pitch:** "Your Databricks plus Anthropic plus OpenAI spend is growing fast. Wolfpack Instinct routes those calls intelligently and cuts 50-80% of the cost without touching the outcomes. We use it ourselves at Wolfpack. We will audit your AI spend in two weeks for free and show you the numbers."

**Why this lands:**
- New CEO has fresh budget scrutiny. Lower AI costs go straight to margin.
- They market themselves as "AI-native." If they actually pay full freight on every AI call, their margins are worse than they need to be. We solve that.
- This is the lowest-stakes, lowest-friction entry point. They are not signing a multi-year deal. They are running an audit on their own infrastructure.

**Asking price:** Free AI spend audit. Paid Instinct deployment if the audit shows >$10K monthly savings. Pricing: base $999-1,999 monthly plus 30-50% of measured savings, capped.

**Time to first signal:** 30 days from intro to audit report.

---

### Play 2: Channel partnership — Instinct white-labeled for OneMagnify's enterprise client base

**The pitch:** "Your clients (Ford, Syngenta, Travis Mathew) all face the same AI cost growth problem. You can offer Instinct as part of your AI consulting practice without building any of it. We do the product; you do the trust and distribution. Revenue share on every deployment."

**Why this lands:**
- They already sell AI consulting and analytics services. Instinct fits inside that.
- They have warm enterprise distribution we cannot build alone in 12 months. Each of their clients is a 5-10x larger contract than our direct-sales motion can reach.
- They get differentiation against other marketing agencies: "We deliver outcomes AND we save you money on the AI infrastructure underneath."

**Asking price:** Revenue share. Suggested 60/40 to OneMagnify on first-year contracts they originate (they take the bigger share to incentivize selling), 50/50 on renewals.

**Time to first deal:** 60-90 days after partnership signing.

---

### Play 3: Wolfpack Auto sold through their FordDirect relationship

**The pitch:** "Your FordDirect work proves you understand the dealer network. The next layer of value to that network is the dealer operating system underneath, where every deal lives. Wolfpack Auto's compliance engine catches TILA, FCRA, FTC dealer-rule violations before they cost dealers fines. The overlay product sits on top of CDK and Reynolds without requiring migration. Together they give Ford an AI dealer experience that no DMS vendor can match."

**Why this lands:**
- FordDirect is exactly Wedge H (OEM-direct) territory in our GTM strategy. OneMagnify can warm-intro us at FordDirect.
- They handle the marketing and customer-facing layer. We handle the operational and compliance layer. No overlap. Clear complement.
- OneMagnify gets to bring an AI-native dealer-operations capability to FordDirect without building it themselves. We get OEM access we cannot buy.

**Asking price:** OneMagnify gets account-management primary on the FordDirect relationship. Wolfpack handles product and implementation. Revenue split tied to the FordDirect contract structure (likely 70/30 to OneMagnify since they own the relationship, but with implementation services going to Wolfpack at near-100% margin).

**Time to first conversation:** 30 days. Time to a real Ford pilot: 6-9 months (enterprise sales cycle).

---

### Play 4: Wolfpack LMS bundled into OneMagnify's Channel Solutions practice

**The pitch:** "Your Channel Solutions work for clients like Syngenta is about training and enabling partner / retailer / dealer networks. That is exactly the persistence problem LMS solves. Bundle LMS into every Channel Solutions engagement as the training and certification layer. Their dealers stay current on new programs. The data feeds back into the analytics work you already do."

**Why this lands:**
- They already sell to channel and partner networks. They already have the buyer in the room.
- LMS as a separate product is in a crowded category. As an embedded capability inside a Channel Solutions contract, it is differentiated and high-margin.
- We get distribution without building a sales team for LMS.

**Asking price:** Internal cost-pass-through plus 25-50% margin on LMS seats inside their client contracts.

**Time to first deployment:** 60 days after partnership signing.

---

### Play 5: Wolfpack Sites CI/CD spine as their digital experience delivery backbone

**The pitch:** "You build websites, eCommerce, and content for major brands. Your delivery cost and time-to-launch is constrained by traditional CI/CD pipelines. We have built an agentic CI/CD spine that auto-generates, auto-tests, and auto-deploys client sites. License it as the operational backbone for your digital experience practice. Your delivery margin goes up. Your time-to-launch goes down."

**Why this lands:**
- They explicitly market "Digital Experience" as a service line but probably ship sites the same way every other agency does.
- The agentic CI/CD work Wolfpack already runs internally is genuinely faster than industry standard. This is a credible 30-50% delivery cost reduction story for them.
- This is a B2B SaaS sale into an agency. Different category from anything else in this brief. Optional, not core.

**Asking price:** $50-150K annual license, plus per-deployment usage fees.

**Time to first deployment:** 90 days after partnership signing.

## Recommended sequence

Lead with Play 1 (Instinct audit). It is free, low-friction, and produces a hard ROI number in two weeks. Whether or not anything else happens, OneMagnify becomes an Instinct customer with a published savings number, which gives Wolfpack credibility we cannot manufacture any other way.

If Play 1 lands, expand to Play 2 (channel partnership) within 60 days. This is the highest-leverage outcome of the relationship.

In parallel with Play 2, raise Play 3 (FordDirect intro) as a warm conversation, not a hard pitch. Let it develop on its own pace; OEM sales cycles are long.

Plays 4 and 5 are smaller, optional expansions to surface later once the first three are in motion.

---

## The conversation to have

The right first conversation is between the Wolfpack CEO and his contact at OneMagnify. The structure:

1. Warm catch-up. (CEO knows how to do this.)
2. Mention that Wolfpack has been quietly building AI infrastructure that solves an expensive problem their industry is starting to feel: AI cost growth. Soften it: "Curious if you're seeing the same thing."
3. Offer a free audit. Two weeks. No commitment. We use Wolfpack Instinct on their actual workloads and show them the numbers.
4. Suggest a follow-up call between OneMagnify's analytics or platform leader and Nick Homyk (CTO) to scope the audit.

Do not pitch all five plays in the first conversation. Land Play 1 first. Earn the right to Plays 2-5 by showing measurable value on Play 1.

## Risk and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| They see Wolfpack as competition (AI services overlap) | Frame as infrastructure layer, not consulting overlap. We provide the tools they sell on top of. |
| The new CEO does not yet have partnership authority | The Wolfpack CEO's contact provides the political cover. Confirm the contact is the right decision-maker before the audit. |
| Our audit shows less savings than promised | Be conservative in the pitch (cite "50-80%" as the range, not the floor). Set audit expectations as "wide range possible." If our actual ML routing on their workload saves only 30%, that is still a real number worth selling. |
| They run the audit and then build it themselves | The audit shares the savings number and a summary methodology, NOT the specific routing thresholds, prompt libraries, or implementation details. Trade-secret separation is the moat, not patents. (Underlying techniques have meaningful prior art, so patenting them is not a real defense.) |
| FordDirect intro conflict with our own Wedge H ambitions | The FordDirect relationship is OneMagnify's. We do not approach Ford direct around them. Channel relationship rules apply. |

If Play 1 lands at a typical OneMagnify-sized customer: $50-150K ARR from Instinct deployment, plus a published case study.

If Play 2 lands: $500K-2M of ARR within 12 months from OneMagnify's enterprise channel.

If all five plays land: $3-10M of incremental ARR within 18 months, plus distribution into the auto OEM and enterprise marketing markets that we cannot buy any other way.

The opening cost: one CEO conversation, two weeks of audit work, a free deliverable to their team. That is the cheapest customer acquisition cost we will ever see for a deal this size.
