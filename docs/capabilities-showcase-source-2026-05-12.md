## Page 3 — The technology layer

### Visual direction
Visual diagram — three or four icons or simple shapes showing how the technology surfaces work together. NOT an architecture diagram. NOT engineering jargon. Should be readable by a brand-marketing VP in 30 seconds.

### Content

**Hero line:**
> We don't bolt AI onto your brand. We engineer it into the experience.

**Capability blocks (3-4 cards Megan designs as visual modules):**

1. **Wolfpack Assistant**
> A natural-language AI layer that runs on top of any platform a brand already uses. Employees and customers interact through conversation; the system learns your business over time and gets smarter the more it's used. Replaces a dozen disconnected settings pages with one assistant that knows your brand.

2. **Intelligent Experience Touchpoints**
> Connected QR, NFC, RFID, and mobile touchpoints that link physical moments to digital follow-through in real time. A customer scans a product on-shelf; the assistant remembers what they cared about; the next interaction is personalized without feeling intrusive.

3. **AgenticQA — continuous platform validation**
> A multi-agent AI system that watches your software stack 24/7 and surfaces problems before customers see them. Built in-house at Wolfpack. Has discovered real security vulnerabilities in production software at companies most consumers know by name.

4. **Adaptive Learning + Insight Systems**
> Every interaction with a Wolfpack-built system feeds a learning loop. The platform observes what works, refines what doesn't, and helps your teams act on patterns you wouldn't otherwise see.

## Page 6 — What we could do together (PROSPECT-SPECIFIC — this page is templated)

### Visual direction
Each concept gets a card with: title, 2-sentence description, one supporting visual (icon or sketch). Four concepts maximum.

### Content — Beyond Inc / BBB first instance

**Concept 1 — Multi-brand portfolio coherence**
> Beyond Inc operates four consumer brands (BBB, Overstock, BuybuyBaby, Zulily). Each has its own brand voice, visual system, and customer journey — which is both an opportunity and a risk. Wolfpack maps the coherence and friction across the full portfolio and designs a system that lets each brand stay distinctive while compounding into a stronger meta-brand.

**Concept 2 — AI customer engagement ecosystem**
> A natural-language assistant layer that runs alongside Beyond's existing e-commerce stack. Customers get personalized help; internal teams get an AI ops layer that knows their business. Built on the Wolfpack Assistant platform, customized for Beyond's brands.

**Concept 3 — Brand experience modernization**
> Bring premium-brand experience discipline (the kind that earned PCNA two consecutive JD Power #1 rankings) into the Beyond Inc consumer brand portfolio. Brand-voice consistency, visual systems, customer-journey orchestration, and the underlying training programs that scale the experience to every customer touchpoint.

**Concept 4 — Adaptive training and enablement at scale**
> The training programs Wolfpack builds for OEM dealer networks (hundreds of locations, thousands of employees) translate directly to Beyond's customer-service, fulfillment, and brand-experience teams. One curriculum framework, certification system, and performance-measurement layer across the full org.

### Re-customization instructions for future prospects
Page 6 is the ONLY page that changes per prospect. To customize:

## Video script — Max production (60-90 seconds)

### Tone
Premium, confident, brand-led. Not technical. Not aspirational-startup. Closer to Porsche brand video than SaaS demo.

### Script (target 75 seconds — adjust for pacing)

**[0:00-0:08] Opening — visual hook**
*Wide shot or motion graphic. No copy on screen yet.*
> VO: "Products commoditize. Pricing commoditizes."

**[0:08-0:18] The thesis**
*Cut to montage: showroom shot, e-commerce checkout, mobile app, training session, brand campaign frame.*
> VO: "But experience is what people remember. And experience is engineered."

**[0:18-0:35] What Wolfpack is**
*Cuts: team-at-work shots, Megan-designed brand work on screen, Wolfpack Assistant interface frames, PCNA work b-roll if usable.*
> VO: "At Wolfpack, we sit at the intersection of customer experience, brand transformation, training, and technology. We design and deliver Return on Experience for the brands that refuse to be forgettable."

**[0:35-0:55] Proof**
*Visual: JD Power #1 graphic, Porsche brand b-roll if licensed, Wolfpack product surfaces on screen.*
> VO: "Our work has contributed to Porsche's #1 ranking in JD Power CSI and SSI — two consecutive years. The same teams, methodology, and technology now apply to retail and e-commerce brands rethinking what modern customer engagement looks like."

**[0:55-1:10] Capabilities flash**
*Quick cuts: 6-8 brand work samples, AI assistant interface, training session, designed PDFs flipping past.*
> VO: "Customer experience. Brand systems. Adaptive training. AI-enabled engagement. All connected. All built around the customer."

**[1:10-1:15] Close**
*Logo + ROE mark on screen. Single frame, hold.*
> VO: "Wolfpack. Return on Experience."

### Production notes for Max
- Cast: real team members where possible, not actors
- Audio: original score preferred, licensed if needed. Voice talent should match Porsche-brand-quality tier.
- Color grade: brand-tight, not generic-tech-startup
- Deliverable formats: 16:9 master, 9:16 for LinkedIn/social, 1:1 for email embeds
- Length variants: full 75s, 30s cutdown, 15s teaser

## Re-use guide (the "Path B" reusability promise)

After this is produced for BBB, the next prospect requires only:

1. **Page 6 re-customization**: 2-4 hours of CTO + Hoxsie thinking, ~1 hour of Megan re-layout. Same card structure, swapped concepts.
2. **Page 8 re-customization** (if included): 1 hour to identify a specific brand-experience observation about the new prospect.
3. **Video stays as-is**: 75-second master is brand-agnostic and reusable across all outbound.

Estimated time per new prospect after BBB: 4-6 hours total to produce a customized version of this artifact.

Estimated time-saved per prospect vs. building from scratch: 30-40 hours.

The investment in producing the first version pays back after roughly the second prospect.
