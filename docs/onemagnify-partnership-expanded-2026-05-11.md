## The thesis

OneMagnify is the highest-leverage single relationship in Wolfpack's pipeline. Not because of any one play, but because of the compounding value of a fully developed partnership.

OneMagnify checks every box that makes a channel partnership real:

1. Brand-new CEO in his first 90 days, hungry for partnerships that signal strategic direction.
2. India GCC just opened, signaling invest-and-scale posture rather than cost-cut posture.
3. Publicly positioned as "AI-native" but their AI is marketing-flavored, not infrastructure-grade. They need what we have built.
4. Direct relationships in auto (FordDirect), agriculture (Syngenta), and consumer retail (Travis Mathew). Three verticals where Wolfpack has product fit.
5. Tech-stack partnerships with Databricks, Salesforce, Shopify, Optimizely, BigCommerce. They run heavy AI/data infrastructure and pay heavy AI/data bills.
6. ~500 employees globally across multiple offices, all of whom need upskilling, certification, and continuous training. That is a captive LMS audience.
7. Service lines that span Wolfpack's product map: data and analytics (Instinct), digital experience (Wolfpack Sites), eCommerce (Wolfpack Auto online retailing), performance marketing (lead intake and enrichment), channel solutions (LMS), strategy (Wolfpack Method consulting).

There is no other prospect in our pipeline where every Wolfpack product has a credible attach. Most prospects are one-product deals. OneMagnify is potentially a six-product deal.

The right strategic posture is not "land one deal and move on." The right posture is "land one entry deal, then sequentially open five more conversations over twelve months." The total addressable value of a fully developed OneMagnify partnership is in the $5M to $25M ARR range across the first eighteen months. Possibly more.

This document maps the six levels of that partnership, what each level looks like, what triggers progression to the next level, and the honest read on what can go wrong.

## The six-level framework

Treat the OneMagnify relationship as a graduated escalator. Each level is concrete and standalone. Each level earns the right to the next. The CEO does not pitch all six at the first meeting. The CTO does not build for all six on day one. We sequence.

### Level 1: Free AI spend audit (the entry move)

**What we offer:** Two-week AI cost audit of OneMagnify's Databricks and LLM API spend. We run their actual workloads through Instinct's cost-routing layer. We hand them a savings number and a methodology summary. They keep the report whether or not they buy.

**What they pay:** Nothing.

**What we get:** A published case study with OneMagnify's logo attached to a quantified savings number. Credibility we cannot manufacture any other way.

**Time:** 2 weeks of CTO time. 30 days end-to-end from intro to report delivery.

**What triggers Level 2:** They like the savings number, want to deploy Instinct, and are willing to sign a public case-study clause.

This is the entry move documented in detail in the CEO memo. The script is already written. The CEO drops it in his next conversation.

### Level 2: Internal Instinct deployment

**What we offer:** Production deployment of Instinct against OneMagnify's actual AI infrastructure. Base subscription plus a share of measured savings, capped. Their internal AI cost falls 50-80 percent. The savings are measured monthly with transparent dashboards.

**What they pay:** Base $1,999-4,999 per month (enterprise tier) plus 30 percent of measured savings, capped at 3x the base tier. Realistic year-one ARR from OneMagnify alone: $80K-300K depending on their AI workload size.

**What we get:** Recurring revenue. A second case study (the production deployment one). Internal champions inside OneMagnify who understand Instinct's value.

**Time:** 30-60 days from Level 1 acceptance to production deployment.

**What triggers Level 3:** They have run Instinct for 60 days and are happy. The internal champion now believes our product is real. The conversation naturally turns to "could our clients use this too?"

### Level 3: Channel partnership for Instinct

**What we offer:** OneMagnify resells Instinct to their enterprise client base. They wrap it inside their AI consulting practice ("we will reduce your AI spend 50-80 percent as part of our AI optimization engagement"). They handle sales and account management. We handle product and implementation. Joint go-to-market.

**What they pay:** Revenue share. Suggested 60/40 to OneMagnify on first-year contracts they originate (they take the larger share to incentivize selling), 50/50 on renewals. White-label optional but not required; positioning "powered by Wolfpack Instinct" works either way.

**What we get:** Access to OneMagnify's enterprise client base. Each customer they bring is 5-10x larger than a customer we could close direct. Realistic year-one ARR through OneMagnify channel: $500K-2M.

**Time:** 30-90 days to formalize the partnership after Level 2 lands. First channel deal: 90-180 days.

**What triggers Level 4:** Channel has produced 3-5 enterprise customers and is generating real revenue. OneMagnify leadership sees Wolfpack as a strategic asset rather than a vendor.

### Level 4: OneMagnify internal transformation

**What we offer:** Wolfpack's product suite deployed internally across OneMagnify's own operations. Not just Instinct, but the full stack.

- **Wolfpack LMS** as their internal learning and certification platform for ~500 employees globally. Onboarding new hires, change management, certification tracking, continuing education. Replaces whatever they have today (likely Workday Learning, Cornerstone, or Docebo).
- **Wolfpack Sites CI/CD spine** as the operational backbone for their Digital Experience practice. Their delivery cost drops, their time-to-launch drops. They get faster project margins.
- **AgenticQA internal pipeline** if applicable to their delivery work. Their multi-agent QA + CI tooling stack drops in cost and gains capability.
- **Wolfpack Auto (Wedge E compliance engine)** if any of their financial-services clients have compliance scanner needs (TILA, FCRA, dealer rule). Sold under their brand.

**What they pay:** Multi-product enterprise license. Realistic annual contract value: $400K-1.2M depending on scope. Likely structured as a single integrated agreement with annual escalators.

**What we get:** A reference enterprise customer that consumes nearly the full Wolfpack product line. Operational telemetry data from their use that improves all our products. Marquee logo for every other sales conversation.

**Time:** 60-120 days of structured procurement after Level 3 starts producing revenue.

**What triggers Level 5:** Internal deployment is humming. OneMagnify's account leadership now uses Wolfpack tools daily and would not switch. The relationship has graduated from vendor to strategic infrastructure partner.

### Level 5: Joint go-to-market across all OneMagnify client verticals

**What we offer:** White-labeled Wolfpack product suite, fully integrated into OneMagnify's service offerings across every vertical they serve:

- **Auto.** FordDirect joint pursuit (Wolfpack Auto under OneMagnify's account ownership). Plus Wolfpack Weekend microsite experiences delivered through OneMagnify's marketing services to FordDirect and other automotive accounts.
- **Agriculture / partner ecosystems.** Wolfpack LMS bundled into every Channel Solutions engagement (Syngenta-style partner enablement programs need persistent training infrastructure). Plus Instinct for the analytics workloads that drive partner-incentive personalization.
- **Consumer retail / eCommerce.** Wolfpack Sites for digital-experience delivery. Plus Instinct for the AI workloads that drive Shopify, BigCommerce, and Optimizely personalization.
- **Cross-vertical compliance.** Wolfpack's compliance engine sold to any client in regulated industries (financial services, healthcare, etc.).

This is OneMagnify's distribution multiplied by Wolfpack's product depth. Their clients buy AI-native marketing services from OneMagnify and unknowingly buy Wolfpack infrastructure underneath.

**What they pay:** Revenue share across all white-label deployments. Tiered: higher share to OneMagnify on the first 10 deployments per product, gradually shifting toward 50/50 at scale.

**What we get:** Distribution into hundreds of enterprise accounts we could never reach directly. Realistic year-one ARR through Level 5 channel: $2M-8M, growing from there.

**Time:** Continuous over 9-18 months. Each vertical light up sequentially as the infrastructure matures.

**What triggers Level 6:** OneMagnify is so reliant on Wolfpack infrastructure that strategic alignment becomes a board-level conversation rather than a procurement one.

### Level 6: Strategic alliance

**What we offer:** A formal strategic alliance positioning OneMagnify as Wolfpack's primary North American channel partner across all verticals where their distribution applies. Possible elements:

- **Co-branded methodology.** "The Wolfpack Method" delivered through OneMagnify in markets and verticals where OneMagnify owns the relationship. We become the technology behind a recognized agency brand.
- **Joint product development.** They surface client needs, we build product. Co-developed roadmap with quarterly alignment.
- **Joint executive presence.** OneMagnify executives appear in Wolfpack pitches. Wolfpack executives appear in OneMagnify pitches. Shared CRM data on overlapping opportunities.
- **Possible minority equity exchange.** OneMagnify takes a minority position in Wolfpack as a strategic investor. Wolfpack possibly takes a small minority position in OneMagnify or signs revenue-share warrants. Aligned cap-table incentives.
- **Joint hiring and offshoring.** OneMagnify's Chennai GCC capacity becomes Wolfpack's nearshore engineering and customer-success extension. We staff from their pool at a preferential rate. They get utilization on capacity that would otherwise sit idle.
- **Exclusive partnerships in certain verticals.** If FordDirect chooses Wolfpack-via-OneMagnify, both parties commit to not competing against each other in that account.

**What they pay:** Strategic terms varying by element. Likely multi-million-dollar contract value annually. Likely involves equity exchange or revenue-share warrants in addition to fees.

**What we get:** A genuine strategic moat. A relationship that is structurally difficult for any competitor to disrupt because we are embedded in their service delivery, their employee training, their go-to-market, and possibly their cap table.

**Time:** 12-24 months from Level 1 if everything compounds. The conversation might begin organically once Level 5 is producing meaningful revenue for both parties.

## Sequencing rules (the discipline)

The temptation will be to pitch too much too fast. Do not. Specific rules:

1. **First conversation: Level 1 only.** Free audit. No other product mentioned. Earn the right to the next conversation by delivering a quantified savings number.

2. **Second conversation (after audit): Level 2 only.** "Here is your savings, here is what production looks like." Get them on as a paying customer. Earn the right to Level 3.

3. **Third conversation (post-deployment, 60 days in): Level 3.** "Your customers face the same problem. Want to offer this through your AI consulting practice?" Get the channel partnership signed. Earn the right to Level 4.

4. **Fourth conversation (after first channel revenue): Level 4 and Level 5 emerge organically.** "We are saving you money on AI. We could save you money on training infrastructure too. Could we deploy our LMS for your 500 employees?" Build out internal use across our product suite. Sequence each product as a separate conversation, not a bundle. The bundle gets resisted by procurement; the per-product additions accumulate naturally.

5. **Level 6 only emerges if Levels 1-5 have established mutual dependence.** Do not pursue an alliance conversation until OneMagnify is already depending on Wolfpack infrastructure in multiple ways. Premature alliance pitches signal desperation and they will be rejected.

6. **Never present the six-level escalator to OneMagnify directly.** This document is internal. Externally, each level looks like the natural next step in a healthy partnership.

## What can go wrong, honest read

**Risk 1: OneMagnify sees Wolfpack as competition rather than infrastructure.**

OneMagnify's AI consulting practice and Wolfpack's product suite overlap conceptually. They might view us as a threat to their own consulting margins rather than as the infrastructure underneath their services. Mitigation: frame Wolfpack as the platform their consultants use to deliver, not as a substitute for their consultants. Their consultants get faster, their margins get better, their value to clients goes up. The framing matters.

**Risk 2: Ravi (new CEO) does not yet have partnership authority.**

He is one month into the role. He may be in listening-and-learning mode rather than decision-making mode. The Wolfpack CEO's personal relationship with him is the political cover. Confirm decision authority before investing CTO time in Level 2.

**Risk 3: India GCC complicates white-label deployment.**

If their offshore team becomes the implementation arm for Wolfpack-powered work, quality control becomes harder. Mitigation: maintain implementation oversight on Wolfpack-deployed work through Wolfpack customer-success rather than fully offshoring it.

**Risk 4: They build internal versions of our products.**

After Level 2, they have seen Instinct's value and could build it themselves. We mitigate this by not sharing implementation specifics during the audit (covered in the CEO memo), by trade secrets, and by always being more advanced than they could economically build internally. Realistic: their internal-build option is more expensive than our partnership, especially as we add LMS, Sites, compliance, etc.

**Risk 5: Acquisition or competitive disruption to OneMagnify itself.**

OneMagnify is a private company. A larger consulting firm (Accenture, Wipro, Cognizant) could acquire them, which would either supercharge our partnership or kill it depending on the acquirer. Mitigation: build the relationship at the operating level not just at the CEO level so it survives leadership changes.

**Risk 6: We bite off more than we can ship.**

Levels 4-5 require us to deliver multi-product enterprise quality across LMS, Sites, Auto compliance, etc. simultaneously. Our small team cannot scale to that without hiring. Mitigation: explicit pause-and-hire decision at Level 3 transition. Do not commit Level 4 SLAs we cannot honor.

## What we offer that no other partner offers

Important to be clear-eyed on why OneMagnify should choose Wolfpack over any other AI infrastructure provider:

1. **Cost-efficiency category leadership.** Anthropic and OpenAI cannot run this play because their revenue depends on AI spend growing, not shrinking. Other AI infrastructure providers (LangChain, Anyscale, Lambda Labs) compete on capability, not cost reduction. We are the only player whose business model aligns with the customer's cost-reduction need.

2. **Multi-product depth.** Most AI infrastructure vendors are single-product. We have a coherent six-product portfolio that maps directly to OneMagnify's six service lines. They cannot buy this alignment anywhere else.

3. **Consulting DNA.** Wolfpack's roots in agency services mean we understand consulting margins, client management, and outcome-based pricing in ways pure product companies do not. OneMagnify's leadership will feel that compatibility immediately.

4. **Engineering velocity.** A 5-person Wolfpack team has shipped multi-month features in days during the period this brief covers. OneMagnify's procurement-to-deploy cycle for traditional vendors is months. Our velocity becomes a tangible competitive advantage in their delivery.

5. **The CEO relationship.** This is the unfair advantage. No other vendor walks in with a warm CEO-to-CEO relationship. The opening conversation is already a yes-to-listening conversation.

## Honest closing

OneMagnify will not be every prospect we land. Most prospects will be one-product deals that we close, deliver, and move on from. OneMagnify is uniquely structured for a multi-product compounding relationship because of how their service lines map to our product portfolio.

If we play this right, OneMagnify becomes our largest single customer relationship in 18 months and the strategic foundation that supports Wolfpack's growth into adjacent verticals through their distribution.

If we play this wrong, the relationship stalls at Level 1 or Level 2 and we get an Instinct customer plus a case study. That is still a positive outcome, but it is a fraction of what is possible.

The discipline matters. Sequence the levels. Earn each one. Do not pitch the escalator. Build the relationship at the operating level so it survives leadership transitions on either side.

The CEO walks into his next OneMagnify conversation with a single one-liner about free AI spend audit. Everything else flows from earning the right to the next conversation.
